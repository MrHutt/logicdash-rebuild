How the board works

Tap a switch to try a different input. Follow the wire to see where that value goes. Your job is to make the lamps behave as each level asks.

Switches
These are the inputs. A switch sends 1 when it’s on and 0 when it’s off.
Wires and sockets
A wire carries a value from OUT to IN. Tap those two sockets in that order to connect them.
Logic gates
A gate reads its inputs and applies a rule. Its OUT can send the result to a lamp or to another gate.
Lamps
A lamp lights when it receives 1 and stays dark when it receives 0. No signal means it has no value coming in yet.

Use What’s happening now to see the values behind the lights. Check step tells you when you’re ready for the next level.

Green carries 1 (on) · Grey carries 0 (off) · Dashed means no value yet
